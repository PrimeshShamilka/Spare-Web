Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Spare (the "App"), published by Syntek ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS.

In short
  • Your budget data (transactions, categories, amounts, notes and your name) is stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive it.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter into the App — your profile name, planned budget, category budgets, transactions, notes, and settings such as currency and month start date — is saved in a local database on your device. This information is not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot access it.

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps).

This information is collected directly by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can control this at any time in your device settings:

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).
  • iOS: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is only available to apps you allow to track you. You can manage this under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which rely on limited data such as general location. You can change your choice by clearing the App's data or reinstalling it, after which you will be asked again.

4. In-app purchases

Where available, the App offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play (under the Google Payments Privacy Notice) or by Apple (under the Apple Privacy Policy). We do not receive your card or billing details. The App only records on your device whether ads have been removed, so it can stop showing them and restore your purchase later.

5. How long information is kept

Data stored by the App stays on your device until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, at which point it is permanently removed. We cannot recover it for you, because we never have a copy. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The only third parties that receive any data in connection with the App are Google (for ads and Google Play purchases) and Apple (for App Store purchases), as described above.

7. Security

Your budget data is protected by your device's own security (such as your screen lock and the operating system's app sandboxing). Because it never leaves your device through the App, it is not exposed to us or to network transmission by the App.

8. Children

The App is intended for adults managing their personal budget and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the App, contact us at support@example.com.
